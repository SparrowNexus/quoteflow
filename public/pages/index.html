Frequently asked questions

Good to know before getting started.

What is QuoteFlow AI?

QuoteFlow AI is a quotation and inventory management application for organising product records, customer information and quotations, including quotation PDF generation.

Is QuoteFlow AI sold as a subscription?

The intended model is a one-time licence. Final licence duration, permitted use, support and other terms will be confirmed in the written licence agreement before purchase.

Can I request a demonstration or licence details?

Yes. Use the enquiry form to request a demo, ask about a licence, or describe your business requirements.

Does the website include an online checkout?

No. The first release uses an enquiry-led process. Payment and automatic licence provisioning are not represented as available.

Where do I log in?

The application login destination will be configured when the production application URL is available. Until then, the Log In link explains that access is not yet configured.
