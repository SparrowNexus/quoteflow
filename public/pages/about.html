About QuoteFlow AI

Make the business of quoting more organised.

QuoteFlow AI is a quotation and inventory management application built around the practical tasks that connect product information, customer records and sales quotations.

Why it exists

Many product-based businesses prepare quotes repeatedly, drawing on product prices, customer details, quantities and document templates. QuoteFlow AI aims to bring these essentials into a focused workflow, helping teams prepare quotations and retrieve their records more consistently.

What guides the product

  • Clarity: keep the information required for a quotation organised.
  • Practicality: focus on real business tasks rather than unnecessary complexity.
  • Transparency: describe capabilities and licence terms accurately, without unsupported promises.
