Refund, Cancellation and Renewal Policy

Draft policy for QuoteFlow AI licence purchases. Final terms require business confirmation and legal review.

Draft for review — see publication requirements on this page.

1. Current purchase process

The public website uses a licence-enquiry process. It does not currently claim to take payments or automatically issue licences.

2. Refund eligibility

No refund window, non-refundable rule or exception is stated until the seller confirms the commercial policy and it has been reviewed for compliance with applicable law. Any written quote or final licence agreement must state the applicable refund conditions before payment.

3. Cancellation and renewal

The intended model is a one-time licence. Whether ongoing maintenance, hosting, optional support or renewals are separately charged must be specified in the written offer. Do not assume recurring billing or automatic renewal.

4. How to request assistance

Email quoteflowai@gmail.com with the enquiry or transaction reference, if one exists.
